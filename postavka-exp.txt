POSTAVKA EKSPERIMENATA
Master rad — paralelizacija RL treniranja sa Ray RLlib
Izvor hiperparametara: config/experiments.yaml (jedina definicija, isto lokalno i na GCP)
GCP yaml-ovi opisuju samo mašine, ne trening.

==============================================================================
1. CILJ
==============================================================================

Izmeriti kako paralelni env_runner-i (workeri) utiču na:
  - throughput (koraci okruženja / sekund)
  - speedup     T(w=1) / T(w=N)
  - efikasnost  speedup / N
  - kvalitet učenja (eval mean ± std, GIF)

Porediti sinhroni on-policy (PPO), asinhroni on-policy (APPO) i off-policy
(DQN / SAC) na tri Gymnasium okruženja rastuće težine.

==============================================================================
2. MATRICA (šta se pokreće)
==============================================================================

Okruženja:
  cartpole        CartPole-v1        diskretne akcije, cilj 450
  lunarlander     LunarLander-v3     diskretne akcije, cilj 200
  bipedalwalker   BipedalWalker-v3   kontinualne akcije, cilj 300

Algoritmi × env:

                 PPO    APPO    DQN    SAC
  CartPole        da     da      da     —
  LunarLander     da     da      da     —
  BipedalWalker   da     da      —      da

Workeri:
  Lokalno:                    1, 2, 4
  GCP CartPole / LunarLander: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16
  GCP BipedalWalker:          1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32

  w=16 na CartPole/LunarLander (16 vCPU, jedna VM): očekivana saturacija —
  16 runnera + learner na 16 jezgara. To je namerna tačka na krivi speedup-a.
  w=16 na BipedalWalker: 2× c3-highcpu-8; w=32: 4× c3-highcpu-8.

Jedan "pun GCP run" = 1 seed × sva polja matrice gore.
Broj treninga u jednom punom runu:
  CartPole:      3 algo × 5 worker = 15
  LunarLander:   3 algo × 5 worker = 15
  BipedalWalker: 3 algo × 6 worker = 18
  Ukupno: 48 treninga po seedu

==============================================================================
5. HIPERPARAMETRI (sažetak; puni u config/experiments.yaml)
==============================================================================

PPO — CartPole (ppo_defaults):
  lr 3e-4, gamma 0.99, clip 0.2, train_batch_size 4000

PPO — LunarLander:
  lr 3e-4, gamma 0.999, gae 0.98, ent 0.01, clip 0.2
  batch 16384, minibatch 64, 4 epohe

PPO — BipedalWalker:
  lr 3e-4, gamma 0.999, gae 0.95, ent 0.0, clip 0.18
  batch 65536, minibatch 4096, 10 epoha, normalize_obs

APPO (nije PPO klon; Ray 2.56 zahteva vtrace=True):
  clip 0.4, lr 5e-4, 1 SGD epoch, V-trace
  CartPole batch 4000; LunarLander 16384; BipedalWalker collect 65536 / learn 16384
  PPO clip 0.2 na async podacima seče update — zato APPO clip 0.4

DQN — CartPole (RLlib-stabilan, ne SB3 Zoo lr=0.0023+128 SGD):
  lr 5e-4, batch 32, buffer 50k, train_freq 4, n_step 3
  eps 1.0 → 0.01 za 50k koraka, target update 500
  Napomena: kolona Reward tokom treninga je ε-greedy; gledati Evolution / eval.

DQN — LunarLander (bliže SB3 Zoo):
  lr 6.3e-4, batch 128, buffer 50k, train_freq 4
  eps 1.0 → 0.1 za 12k, target 250

SAC — samo BipedalWalker:
  lr 7.3e-4, gamma 0.98, tau 0.02, batch 256, buffer 300k
  64 gradient_steps / 1024 env koraka, net [400, 300], normalize_obs
  RLlib nema gSDE; MeanStdFilter je obavezan

Eval posle svakog treninga: 10 greedy epizoda (mean ± std) + best GIF.

==============================================================================
7. KOMANDE — JEDAN PUN GCP RUN (1 seed)
==============================================================================

CartPole + LunarLander:
  ray up gcp/ray_cluster_lunarlander.yaml
  ray attach gcp/ray_cluster_lunarlander.yaml
  # Na VM:
  cd ~/master-rad
  python src/run_experiments.py --envs cartpole lunarlander \
      --algo ppo appo dqn --workers 1 2 4 8 16 --gif --evaluate 10 --monitor \
      | tee results/run.log
  # Sa laptopa, PRE ray down:
  mkdir -p results/gcp
  ray rsync-down gcp/ray_cluster_lunarlander.yaml ~/master-rad/results/ ./results/gcp/
  ray down gcp/ray_cluster_lunarlander.yaml

BipedalWalker:
  ray up gcp/ray_cluster_bipedalwalker.yaml
  ray attach gcp/ray_cluster_bipedalwalker.yaml
  # Na VM:
  cd ~/master-rad
  python src/run_experiments.py --envs bipedalwalker \
      --algo ppo appo sac --workers 1 2 4 8 16 32 --gif --evaluate 10 --monitor \
      | tee results/run.log
  mkdir -p results/gcp
  ray rsync-down gcp/ray_cluster_bipedalwalker.yaml ~/master-rad/results/ ./results/gcp/
  ray down gcp/ray_cluster_bipedalwalker.yaml

Obavezno: prvo rsync-down, pa ray down. ray down briše VM i sve rezultate.
Rezultati na head-u: ~/master-rad/results/  (JSON, gifs/, checkpoints/, summary_*.json)
file_mounts šalju samo src/ i config/ na klaster — rezultate moraš ručno da povučeš.

Lokalno (isti yaml, manje workera):
  python src/run_experiments.py --envs cartpole \
      --algo ppo appo dqn --workers 4 --gif --evaluate 10

9. KOLIKO RUNOVA ZA RAD
==============================================================================

Tema je skalabilnost, ne SOTA nagrada.

  1) Jedan pun GCP run (1 seed, matrica iz §2)
     → tabele speedup / throughput / efikasnost, GIF-ovi, eval.
     To je glavni rezultat.

  2) Lokalni w=1,2,4 (deo već postoji)
     → poređenje laptop vs GCP.

  3) Opciono: 3 seeda SAMO na jednoj širini (w=4 ili w=8), svi algo
     → ako u radu piše "PPO dostiže X ± Y".
     Ne ponavljati celu matricu 3×.

Ne treba 3 × 48 treninga.
